# Environments — boards, backdrops, splash and key-art

Scope: loading/title paintings (T-*), bundle key-art (BND-*), event boards,
screen head-pieces (the UI3D-bg-* class: hall, armoury, market, drill yard,
road…), season splashes. The world MAP itself is 3D terrain — backdrop
tiles are not generated here.

## Rules

- Aspect per destination: screen head-pieces are wide strips (state the
  crop intent: "composed for a wide banner crop, subject centred in the
  middle band"); title/loading 16:9; bundle cards per the shop card.
- **Layered depth**: foreground anchor → subject band → atmospheric haze;
  the master block's torchlight/sun from upper-left holds across layers.
- **UI safe zones stated in the prompt**: the game lays chrome over these
  paintings — "the lower quarter and right edge stay quiet: low contrast,
  no focal detail, room for interface panels."
- The realm's geography vocabulary (from the shipped map and boards):
  green river heartlands under two rivers; stone hill country; dark pine
  marches; the barbarians' ash-and-hide camps. Original geography only.
- Time-of-day presets: campaign dawn (parchment sky, long cool shadows);
  working noon (full warm key); siege dusk (ember rim on stone, first
  torches); chronicle night (lantern pools, ink-blue dark).
- **Money-law**: war imagery must never sit over a buy button (two bundle
  paintings were refused for exactly this and routed to earned surfaces).
  Bundle art for PAID surfaces shows goods, works and journeys — not
  battle.
- No text anywhere (titles are set by the game), no borders.

## Ready fragments

- **loading vignette**: FIRST LIGHT ON THE PASS: a ruined watchtower on a
  saddle of rock, one banner streaming, dawn parchment sky, the road
  switchbacking down into green heartlands under thin mist, lower quarter
  quiet for interface.
- **armoury head-piece**: THE ARMOURY WALL: racked arms in warm lamplight,
  an anvil worn bright at the centre band, quench steam drifting right,
  composed for a wide banner crop with the lower third quiet.
- **season splash (siege dusk)**: THE SEASON OF SIEGES: a curtain wall at
  dusk, ember rim-light on the stone, mantlets creeping in silhouette
  below, torches pricking the hoardings, ink-blue night rolling in from
  the right.
